This is some text to demonstrate how floated elements interact with other content. Notice how each line is shortened to fit the narrow channel that remains between the two floated boxes: the left box sits against the left edge, the right box against the right edge, and the text flows down the middle. The lines stay narrow line after line until the text passes the bottom of the taller floated box. After that point the lines reclaim the full width of the container and resume their normal flow below the floats, exactly as they would if no floats were present at all.

Explanation

This example demonstrates the CSS float property:

  • The left blue box has float: left;
  • The right blue box has float: right;

Notice how:

  • The two boxes are pulled out of the block flow and pinned to the left and right edges of the container.
  • The paragraph stays in normal flow, and its lines wrap into the channel between the boxes.
  • Once the text clears the bottom of the boxes, it returns to full width.
  • The container has display: flow-root; (a modern clearfix) so it grows to contain the floated boxes instead of collapsing.
